\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented the Envelope Equivariant Graph Policy with Neighborhood Proximal Allocation (EGP-NPA) framework for cooperative deadlock resolution of multi-robot systems subject to mutually conflicting global connectivity and pairwise collision-avoidance scalar constraints. First, an exact rational second-order KKT certificate on a seven-robot symmetric contact configuration (Theorem~\ref{thm:seven-kkt}) proved that safe and connected reachability does not imply reachability under myopic error-monotone safety filters, identifying lateral symmetry-breaking drive and dynamic quota-driven neighbor yielding as key physical mechanisms for deadlock resolution. Second, we developed an $\mathrm{SO}(2)\ltimes\mathbb R^2$ and permutation-equivariant GNN residual nominal policy (Proposition~\ref{prop:equivariance}), proving why preserving $\mathrm{SO}(2)$ chiral orientation over full $\mathrm{O}(2)$ reflection equivariance is essential to prevent zero tangential output on symmetric head-on encounters. Third, we established anytime continuous-time safety invariance (Proposition~\ref{prop:safety}), derived the closed-form solution for cooperative unfreezing of stationary neighbors via dynamic quota transfer (Proposition~\ref{prop:unfreeze}), and proved first-order $\mathcal O(\norm{\be_k}_2)$ residual suppression and goal equilibrium recovery under the stateless one-hop completion envelope $\rho_{i,k}$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:envelope}). Across $96$ training-scale and zero-shot cross-scale benchmarks and $48$ independent holdout instances, EGP-NPA achieved $100\%$ safe arrival and zero safety violations ($90.6\%$ and $87.5\%$ cross-scale arrival under matched half-budget two-seed retraining)—outperforming shield-consistent safe MARL ($53.1\%,p=6.1\times 10^{-5}$), static-split CBF shielded GNN ($3.1\%,p=9.3\times 10^{-10}$), task-adapted DGPPO ($0\%$ violation-free arrival), primal-dual MAPPO-Lagrangian ($0\%$ violation-free arrival), tangential circulation heuristics ($40.6\%,p=3.8\times 10^{-6}$), and myopic CBF filtering ($15.6\%,p=1.5\times 10^{-8}$)—while second-order \texttt{MuJoCo} simulations with online division-free spectral estimation validated full-stack integration and characterized the physical safety-margin versus channel-passability trade-off (Theorem~\ref{thm:passability-tradeoff}). Future work will incorporate multi-scale boundary-clearance features and multi-seed curriculum training on larger lattices, as well as extensions to nonholonomic and field-of-view-constrained multi-robot teams.
